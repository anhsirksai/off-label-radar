Off-Label Radar

Every active trial × every FDA label, judged by Jev. Finds approved drugs being tested outside their labeled indications — the CT.gov-vs-DailyMed diff nobody publishes.

How to read it: P(off-label) is Jev's probability the trial targets something the label doesn't cover. Plausibility (1–5) is how strong the supplied PubMed evidence is. Click a row to see the label text, the trial, the abstracts, and every probability Jev returned. Research tool, not medical advice — verify against the current label.

nouloff_label — "Is this trial testing the drug outside its approved Indications & Usage?" (label text verbatim + trial) → P(off-label) column; gate at 0.6, below that no evidence is pulled.
noulis_active_arm — "Is the drug the studied intervention, not a comparator / background therapy?" → comparator? flag; multiplies the rank.
scoreplausibility — 5-level rubric (incidental → RCT evidence) judged on the top-5 PubMed abstracts → Plausibility column (1–5).
choicecategory — new_indication / label_extension / combination / unclear → Category badge; full distribution in the drawer.
choicecondition_match (condition mode) — "Which MeSH term is what the user typed?" over the terms CT.gov returned → shown above the results as you asked X, we searched Y.
Program flow / decision tree — how a drug or condition becomes a ranked candidate

Finds the newest active trials for the condition, pulls every drug they test, resolves each to its FDA label, and Jev splits them into label already covers it vs being tested off-label. Hot conditions have thousands of trials — only the newest N are scanned.
